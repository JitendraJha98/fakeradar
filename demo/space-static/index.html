Is this image AI-generated? This page runs the fakeradar fast-v0 model inside your browser. Your image never leaves your computer.

Read before trusting a score. The model was trained on one generator (Stable Diffusion v1.4). It is strong on that family, weaker on others, and was never tested on 2025-2026 commercial generators. Real photos can score high: in a spot check one real photo scored 0.80. A score is a probabilistic signal, never proof about a person. For real decisions, calibrate a threshold on your own real photos with fakeradar calibrate-fpr.

How it works: up to four full-resolution 256×256 squares are cut from the image (centre first, then corners, never shrunk), each is scored by the model, and the scores are averaged. Below 0.35 reads "likely real", above 0.65 "likely AI", in between "uncertain". Models: fakeradar-fast · fakeradar-accurate (more robust, run it locally) · code: github.com/JitendraJha98/fakeradar.
